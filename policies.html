01

Cancellation & Refunds

This applies to both private coaching sessions and workshop registrations.

  • You can reschedule through Cal.com at any time before your session.
  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before your session receive a full refund.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before your session are not eligible for a refund. This includes no-shows.
  • If we need to cancel or reschedule a session, for illness, an emergency, or anything else, you'll get a full refund or a free reschedule, whichever you'd rather have.
  • If a workshop doesn't reach minimum enrollment and we need to cancel or reschedule it, everyone registered gets a full refund or the option to move to the next date.

The 24-hour line exists because a late cancellation or no-show holds a spot another actor could have used, especially in a capped workshop. It's not meant to be punitive; if something truly out of your control comes up, reach out and we'll work with you.

02

Code of Conduct

This applies to private sessions and group workshops, on Zoom or in person.

  • We work to create a focused, respectful, harassment-free space for ourselves and from everyone else in the room.
  • Feedback in a session is about the work: choices, habits, technique. It is never a judgement of you as a person, and we ask for the same respect in return, toward us and toward other actors in a group setting.
  • Group workshops run on mutual trust and respect. What's shared in the room stays in the room; see the Confidentiality section below.
  • We reserve the right to end a session, or remove someone from a workshop, without a refund, if their conduct is abusive, discriminatory, or makes the space unsafe for us or for another participant.

03

Privacy

This site does not run analytics or tracking scripts, and it doesn't use cookies to track you across visits.

When you book a session, sign up for the email list, or send a message through the site, here's what happens with that information:

  • Booking: Scheduling and payment for sessions and workshops are handled directly by Cal.com. We see your name, email, and whatever you enter in the booking form. We don't handle or store your payment details. Payments are handled by Cal.com and Stripe.
  • Contact form and email list: Messages sent through the Reach Out form or the workshop-announcement signup are delivered to us by FormSubmit. We use that information to reply to you and, if you opted in, to let you know about upcoming workshop dates. Nothing you submit is sold or handed off to a third party for any reason.
  • Hosting: The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information (like IP address) the way any web host does. We don't have access to detailed visitor analytics.

We keep contact information only as long as we are working together. You can ask us to delete what we have on file or unsubscribe from announcements at any time by emailing info@averywhitted.com.

04

Confidentiality

Group workshops often involve sharing personal material in front of other actors: self-tape footage, scene work, audition stories, the stuff that makes the feedback useful also makes it personal.

  • By joining a workshop, you're agreeing to keep what's shared there in the room. Don't discuss, describe, screenshot, or repost another participant's work, choices, or personal stories outside the session.
  • We do not share your audition material, submissions, or anything personal you've shared with other actors, casting contacts, or anyone else, without your permission.
  • This is separate from the Recording policy below, which covers whether a session can be recorded at all.

05

Recording
  • We don't record sessions by default. If a recording would be useful, for example, reviewing feedback on a self-tape later, we can agree to it in advance.
  • Group workshops may not be recorded by participants in any form without the explicit consent of everyone involved as well as ourselves.
  • Recording sessions for accessibility reasons is acceptable. However, you must first get explicit permission from ourselves and all participants involved before doing so.

06

Accessibility & Accommodations

Sessions run on Zoom by default, with in-person options available in NYC on request.

  • Zoom's built-in live captions can be turned on for any session, just ask.
  • If there are accommodations that would help you do your best work (E.g. extra processing time, materials in an alternative format, a different session structure, etc...) please reach out any time, and we'd be happy to discuss.
  • For in-person sessions, studio accessibility is confirmed case by case when you book, since space availability varies.

07

Age & Eligibility

Workshops and private coaching are built for actors 18 and older who are actively auditioning or working toward it.

For actors under 18, sessions must be booked with the explicit consent of a parent or guardian. If necessary, a parent or guardian will need to handle booking and communication, and in some cases we may require a parent or guardian to be present for sessions. Please reach out before booking so we can determine what works best.

08

Materials & Intellectual Property
  • Frameworks, exercises, sides, and written materials we provide during coaching or a workshop are for your own personal use. Do not redistribute, resell or repost them without our permission.
  • We may occasionally reference general, anonymized patterns from previous sessions in teaching materials or elsewhere (E.g. "actors often get stuck on X"), but we never share identifying information, or personal stories without permission.
  • Testimonials on this site are used only with the explicit permission of the participant who wrote them.

09

Payment
  • Session rates are subject to change. However, rates will never be changed for sessions after they are booked.
  • Refunds will be issued in the amount that was paid for that session. Current session rates do not influence refunds.
  • Payment is collected through Cal.com at the time of booking.
  • Refunds are issued through Stripe and may take several business days to be processed.
