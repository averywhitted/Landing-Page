90% of the Job

Surviving Auditions as a Working Actor

Most of the life of an actor isn't spent on stage or in front of a camera. It's spent scrolling casting notices, memorizing lines, uploading self-tapes, juggling day jobs, and waiting for something to click.

It's hard out here, but you don't have to go it alone. Join host and fellow working actor Avery Whitted for conversations, stories, and honest advice that demystify the grind and make each audition a little easier.
